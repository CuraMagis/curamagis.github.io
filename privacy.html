Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

CuraMagis ("we," "us," or "our") is a private college admissions counseling practice operated by Katie Burns. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you share with us through curamagis.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Contact and inquiry information — name, email address, and any details you include when you reach out through our contact form or coming-soon sign-up.
  • Communications — the content of any email or message you send us.

We also collect limited information automatically through analytics tools (see below).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry about counseling services.
  • To send you updates you have subscribed to (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • To process payments for counseling services through our payment processor.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous data about site usage — pages visited, time on site, general geographic region, and how visitors arrived. Google Analytics uses cookies. You can opt out using Google's opt-out browser add-on. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

Email Marketing

If you subscribe to email updates, your email address is stored with Kit (ConvertKit). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send. Kit's privacy policy is available at kit.com/privacy.

Payment Processing

Payments for counseling services are processed by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers or payment details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry and client information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To make a request, contact us at billing@curamagis.com.

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to families and adult students. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Parents or guardians who contact us on behalf of a student are responsible for the information they share.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at billing@curamagis.com.
